\begin{center}
{\Large \textit{Kelo v. City of New London}, 545 U.S. 469 (2005)\par}
\vspace{0.3em}
{\normalsize Case Brief --- One-Page Edition\par}
\end{center}

\textbf{Memory Jogger.} Economic development is a ``public purpose'' justifying condemnation of unblighted homes (\href{https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/545/469/}{Justia}).

\textbf{Facts.} Distressed New London adopted a 90-acre redevelopment plan beside a new Pfizer facility and condemned 15 unblighted properties, including Susette Kelo's renovated home and the house where Wilhelmina Dery had lived since her birth in 1918, after most of the 115 owners sold.

\textbf{Procedural History.} The Connecticut Supreme Court, 4--3, upheld the takings under \textit{Berman} and \textit{Midkiff}.

\textbf{Votes.} Affirmed, 5--4: Stevens for the Court; Kennedy concurring; O'Connor (with Rehnquist, Scalia, Thomas) and Thomas dissenting.

\textbf{Holding.} Takings under a comprehensive economic-development plan satisfy the Public Use Clause under deferential review; States may impose stricter limits.

\textbf{Reasoning.} ``Public use'' means public purpose; plans are judged as a whole, and there was no pretext. Kennedy would still void takings favoring a particular private party; O'Connor said the line between public and private use was erased; Thomas would overrule \textit{Berman}.

\textbf{Examples.} \emph{Same-Side:} a studied industrial-park plan. \emph{Opposite-Side:} a one-parcel transfer to a disappointed buyer. \emph{Fence-Sitter:} a plan built around a named team's arena.

\textbf{Critique.} Fair-market compensation omits subjective value and relocation costs, and Kennedy's pretext test is hard to apply when private profit and public tax gains are intertwined.

\textbf{Key Quotation.} ``Promoting economic development is a traditional and long accepted function of government. There is, moreover, no principled way of distinguishing economic development from the other public purposes that we have recognized.'' --- Justice Stevens, 545 U.S.\ at 484.
