\begin{center}
{\Large \textit{Moody v.\ NetChoice, LLC}, 603 U.S.\ 707 (2024)\par}
\vspace{0.3em}
{\normalsize Case Brief --- One-Page Edition\par}
\end{center}

\textbf{Memory Jogger.} Facial challenges to state social-media laws were remanded; feed curation is protected expression (\href{https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/603/22-277/}{Justia}).

\textbf{Facts.} Amid concerns that platforms suppressed conservative views, Florida and Texas in 2021 barred large platforms from ``censoring'' users or posts and required individualized explanations. NetChoice, representing Facebook, YouTube, and others, brought facial challenges.

\textbf{Procedural History.} The Eleventh Circuit blocked Florida's law; the Fifth Circuit upheld Texas's, calling moderation ``not speech.''

\textbf{Votes.} Vacated, 9--0: Kagan for the Court; Barrett, Jackson, Thomas, and Alito concurred.

\textbf{Holding.} Neither court applied the facial standard; curating third-party posts is protected editorial expression.

\textbf{Reasoning.} Courts must map each law's full scope, from feeds to messaging and payment apps, and weigh applications. Under \textit{Tornillo} and \textit{Hurley}, compiling and labeling speech is expressive, and Texas's goal of rebalancing speech fails even intermediate scrutiny. Barrett flagged AI moderation and foreign ownership as open questions; Alito called Part III ``nonbinding dicta'' and invoked common-carrier history.

\textbf{Examples.} \emph{Same-Side:} must-carry for candidates. \emph{Opposite-Side:} payment-app rules. \emph{Fence-Sitter:} engagement-only algorithms.

\textbf{Critique.} The dicta dispute and the algorithm question remain open, and the facial standard raises the cost of pre-enforcement suits.

\textbf{Key Quotation.} ``Deciding on the third-party speech that will be included in or excluded from a compilation---and then organizing and presenting the included items---is expressive activity of its own. And that activity results in a distinctive expressive product.'' --- Justice Kagan, 603 U.S.\ at 731.
