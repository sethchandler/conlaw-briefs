\begin{center}
{\Large \textit{Boumediene v. Bush}, 553 U.S. 723 (2008)\par}
\vspace{0.3em}
{\normalsize Case Brief --- One-Page Edition\par}
\end{center}

\textbf{Memory Jogger.} Habeas runs to Guantanamo; the MCA's jurisdiction-stripping unconstitutionally suspended the writ (\href{https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/553/723/}{553 U.S. 723}).

\textbf{Facts.} After the September 11 attacks, Lakhdar Boumediene, an Algerian-born Bosnian citizen seized in Bosnia, and other foreign nationals captured abroad were held at Guantanamo, where Cuba kept formal sovereignty but the United States had complete control. Tribunals (CSRTs) labeled them enemy combatants without counsel or classified evidence, and MCA \S~7 stripped habeas jurisdiction, leaving limited D.C. Circuit review under the Detainee Treatment Act.

\textbf{Procedural History.} The D.C. Circuit held that \S~7 reached the pending cases and that noncitizens held outside U.S. sovereign territory had no constitutional habeas right. The Supreme Court first denied certiorari, then granted it.

\textbf{Votes.} Reversed 5--4. Kennedy wrote for the Court; Souter concurred; Roberts and Scalia dissented, joined by Thomas and Alito.

\textbf{Holding.} The Suspension Clause has full effect at Guantanamo, and DTA review is no adequate substitute, so \S~7 is an unconstitutional suspension.

\textbf{Reasoning.} ``Questions of extraterritoriality turn on objective factors and practical concerns, not formalism'': status and process, the site of detention, and practical obstacles all favored the detainees, unlike in \textit{Eisentrager}. An adequate substitute must permit new exculpatory evidence and an order of release. Six years of detention excused exhaustion. Roberts said the Court struck the most generous protections ever given enemy combatants without letting the D.C. Circuit test them; Scalia called the test ``judicially brainstormed.''

\textbf{Examples.} \emph{Same-Side:} a leased atoll under exclusive U.S. control. \emph{Opposite-Side:} a base in an active combat zone. \emph{Fence-Sitter:} a facility on an ally's base.

\textbf{Critique.} The functional test gives little guidance on when control becomes \textit{de facto} sovereignty, the Court left the definition of ``enemy combatant'' and the burden of proof open, and \textit{Thuraissigiam} (2020) later confined the writ to its 1789 core.

\textbf{Key Quotation.} ``To hold the political branches have the power to switch the Constitution on or off at will is quite another.'' --- Justice Kennedy, 553 U.S. at 765.
