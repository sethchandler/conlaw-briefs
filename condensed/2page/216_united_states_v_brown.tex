\begin{center}
{\LARGE \textit{United States v.\ Brown}, 381 U.S.\ 437 (1965)\par}
\vspace{0.5em}
{\large Case Brief --- Two-Page Edition\par}
\end{center}

\section{Memory Jogger}
A law barring Communist Party members from union office was a bill of attainder: Congress singled out a named group for punishment instead of enacting a general rule (\href{https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/381/437/}{Justia}).

\section{Detailed Case Facts}
Section 504 of the Labor-Management Reporting and Disclosure Act of 1959 made it a crime for a current or recent (within five years) Communist Party member to serve as a union officer or board member. Archie Brown, an open Party member for over twenty-five years, was elected to the executive board of a San Francisco longshoremen's local.

\section{Procedural History}
Brown was convicted and sentenced to six months, though the government never claimed he had urged a political strike. The en banc Ninth Circuit reversed on First and Fifth Amendment grounds; the Supreme Court affirmed on attainder grounds.

\section{Judicial Votes}
Affirmed 5--4. Warren wrote for Black, Douglas, Brennan, and Goldberg; White dissented, joined by Clark, Harlan, and Stewart.

\section{Holding}
Congress may not single out an easily ascertainable group and punish it without a judicial trial, even for preventive purposes. Section 504 is an unconstitutional bill of attainder.

\section{Analysis of Opinions}
\textbf{Majority:} the Clause implements the separation of powers; Congress must write general rules for courts to apply, not designate a political party as dangerous; membership is no neutral ``shorthand''; and preventive aims are still punishment, repudiating \textit{Douds}. \textbf{White:} the Court substituted a per se ban on specificity for the punishment factors, threatening many regulatory laws.

\section{Examples: Future Applications}
\textbf{Same-Side:} a felony ban on members of a named protest group serving on the boards of utilities. \textbf{Opposite-Side:} disqualification after an individualized hearing with judicial review. \textbf{Fence-Sitter:} a law defunding a named nonprofit.

\section{Critique}
After \textit{Nixon v.\ Administrator of General Services} treated targeted preventive laws as regulation, \textit{Brown} may survive mainly as a bar on criminalizing party membership, and its reasoning may do First Amendment work under a structural label.

\section{Key Quotations}
\begin{quote}
``The best available evidence, the writings of the architects of our constitutional system, indicates that the Bill of Attainder Clause was intended not as a narrow, technical (and therefore soon to be outmoded) prohibition, but rather as an implementation of the separation of powers, a general safeguard against legislative exercise of the judicial function, or, more simply, trial by legislature.''
\end{quote}
\noindent --- Chief Justice Warren, for the Court, 381 U.S.\ at 442.
