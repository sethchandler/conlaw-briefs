{\LARGE \textit{Boumediene v. Bush}, 553 U.S. 723 (2008)\par}
\vspace{0.5em}
{\large Case Brief --- Two-Page Edition\par}
\end{center}

\section{Memory Jogger}
Guantanamo detainees have the constitutional privilege of habeas corpus, and the Military Commissions Act's jurisdiction-stripping was an unconstitutional suspension (\href{https://supreme.justia.com/cases/federal/us/553/723/}{553 U.S. 723}).

\section{Detailed Case Facts}
Lakhdar Boumediene and other foreign nationals captured abroad were held at Guantanamo, where Cuba kept formal sovereignty but the United States had complete control. Military tribunals (CSRTs) classified them as enemy combatants without counsel or access to classified evidence. MCA \S~7 stripped federal habeas jurisdiction, leaving only limited D.C. Circuit review under the Detainee Treatment Act.

\section{Procedural History}
The D.C. Circuit held that noncitizens held outside sovereign U.S. territory had no constitutional habeas right.

\section{Judicial Votes}
Reversed 5--4. Kennedy wrote for Stevens, Souter, Ginsburg, and Breyer; Souter concurred; Roberts and Scalia, each joined by the other dissenters, dissented.

\section{Holding}
The Suspension Clause has full effect at Guantanamo under a functional test, and DTA review is not an adequate substitute for habeas, so MCA \S~7 is an unconstitutional suspension.

\section{Analysis of Opinions}
\textbf{Majority:} ``Questions of extraterritoriality turn on objective factors and practical concerns, not formalism'': the detainees' status and process, the sites of detention, and practical obstacles. An adequate substitute must allow new exculpatory evidence and an order of release. \textbf{Roberts:} the Court struck down the most generous protections ever given enemy combatants. \textbf{Scalia:} the writ never ran to aliens abroad, and the test is ``judicially brainstormed.''

\section{Examples: Future Applications}
\textbf{Same-Side:} a leased atoll under exclusive U.S. control, far from any war. \textbf{Opposite-Side:} a forward base in an active combat zone. \textbf{Fence-Sitter:} a U.S.-run facility on an allied nation's base.

\section{Critique}
The functional test is indeterminate, and the Court left the substantive detention rules open. \textit{Thuraissigiam} (2020) later confined the writ to its 1789 core.

\section{Key Quotations}
\begin{quote}
``To hold the political branches have the power to switch the Constitution on or off at will is quite another.''
\end{quote}
\noindent --- Justice Kennedy, for the Court, 553 U.S. at 765.
