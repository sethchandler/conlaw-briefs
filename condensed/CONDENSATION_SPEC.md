# Condensed editions: rules (pilot version)

Three print editions of every brief, made from the audited full brief in `briefs/`.

## Content discipline (all editions)

- Everything comes from the audited full brief: no new facts, cases, holdings, or characterizations.
- Quotations are copied verbatim from the full brief, which was checked against the official reports. A script confirms every quotation in a condensed brief appears in the full brief.
- Keep the primary-source link in the Memory Jogger; drop the other inline links to save space.
- Keep the analytical spine, in this order of priority: the holding and rule, the vote and opinion lineup, the majority's reasoning, the main separate opinions, examples covering both sides plus a close case, the leading critiques, and quotations.

## Editions

| | 4-page | 2-page | 1-page |
|---|---|---|---|
| Layout | full-brief preamble (12pt, 1in margins, 1.25em paragraph space) | 12pt, no indent, 0.6em paragraph space | 12pt, no indent, 0.45em paragraph space, smaller title |
| Headings | nine `\section`s | nine `\section`s | nine bold run-in labels |
| Judicial Votes | itemized list | one paragraph | one sentence |
| Analysis | majority plus each separate opinion (bold run-in) | one paragraph | combined into Reasoning |
| Examples | all five (2 same-side, 2 opposite-side, 1 fence-sitter), 1–3 sentences each | three: same-side, opposite-side, fence-sitter | three, a phrase each |
| Critique | two short paragraphs | two or three sentences | one sentence |
| Key Quotations | three | one | one |
| Target length | ~1,000 words of source; 4 pages, last page at least half full | ~520 words; 2 pages | ~400 words; 1 page, about 85% full |

Subtitle: "Case Brief --- Four-Page Edition" (or Two-Page / One-Page).

## Acceptance checks

1. Compiles with no errors and no LaTeX or overfull-box warnings.
2. Exactly the target page count.
3. Every quotation of three or more words is verbatim in the full brief.
